RUNBOOK: Billing Issue Resolution
Category: BSS / Billing
Domain: BILLING
Last Updated: 2024-01

== OVERVIEW ==
Billing issues arise from failed invoice generation, incorrect charges, payment failures,
or credit limit breaches. These directly impact customer experience and revenue assurance.

== COMMON ERROR CODES ==

BILL_003 - Credit Limit Exceeded
  Cause: The customer's outstanding balance plus the new charge exceeds their credit limit.
  Resolution Steps:
    1. Query BILLING_ACCOUNT for customer's CREDIT_LIMIT and CURRENT_BALANCE.
    2. Check BILLING_INVOICE for overdue invoices.
    3. If customer has overdue invoices, attempt automated payment via stored payment method.
    4. If payment succeeds, release the order.
    5. If customer is enterprise, escalate to Account Manager for credit review.
    6. Consider temporary credit limit increase for VIP/Enterprise customers.
    7. Log action in INCIDENT_TICKET with CATEGORY = 'BILLING'.

BILL_009 - Payment Method Declined
  Cause: Credit card, ACH, or other payment method was rejected by payment gateway.
  Resolution Steps:
    1. Check BILLING_ACCOUNT for current payment method.
    2. Retry payment after 30 minutes (transient gateway issues).
    3. If retry fails, notify customer via email/SMS to update payment method.
    4. Suspend service after 7-day grace period if no payment update.
    5. Update BILLING_ACCOUNT status to COLLECTIONS if unresolved after 30 days.

BILL_INV_ERR - Invoice Generation Failure
  Cause: Missing charges, orphaned subscriptions, or rating engine failure.
  Resolution Steps:
    1. Verify all USAGE_RECORD entries for the billing period are rated (IS_RATED = 1).
    2. Re-run rating engine for unrated records.
    3. Check for BILLING_CHARGE entries without a linked BILLING_INVOICE.
    4. Re-trigger invoice generation after resolving gaps.
    5. Validate invoice totals against sum of BILLING_CHARGE records.

== REVENUE ASSURANCE CHECKS ==
  - Compare sum(BILLING_CHARGE.AMOUNT) vs BILLING_INVOICE.SUBTOTAL monthly.
  - Flag invoices where TOTAL_AMOUNT = 0 but subscriptions are ACTIVE.
  - Alert on USAGE_RECORD entries older than 48h with IS_RATED = 0.

== ESCALATION PATH ==
  Level 1: Billing Operations
  Level 2: Revenue Assurance Engineering
  Level 3: CFO Office (for disputes > $10,000)
